On Nov. 20, Google added GIF creation to the Gemini app. The feature uses Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, The Verge reported, and turns a text prompt, your own photos, or a video into a short looping clip of about three seconds. According to The Verge, support is rolling out to all users in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Japan, Korea, Australia, Germany, and France.

Google's own help page is a little stricter, and that is the safer list to read before you hunt for the button. For a personal account, it says you must have a Google AI plan, and it points to an upgrade page. Work or school accounts need a qualifying Workspace license. The feature is not available to users under 18. To create GIFs you must be in Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Japan, Korea, the UK, or the US, and the help page notes that the ability is rolling out gradually and may not be on your account yet.

## How to make one, and how to share it

In Gemini Apps, you open the sidebar, choose Create video, and, if you want, pick a template. You can type a prompt, attach files, or do both. Google says you can upload one video and up to five images. You can also brainstorm the wording in the main chat before you generate anything. A clip can take a few minutes, and while it is being made you cannot keep talking in the same chat; you can start a new one and come back later.

Once the GIF is ready, you can copy and paste it into documents, email, messages, and other apps that accept an animated GIF, or download it as a `.gif` file. You can then edit it in the same conversation: remove or replace objects, change a camera angle, or adjust the scene. Google also describes longer regular videos, aspect-ratio controls, and avatars, which are separate from the short loop.

The usual rules apply. You must have the rights to any images you upload, and you should not generate or share clips to deceive, harass, or harm people. A footnote on the help page adds that uploading videos for video edits and for avatar creation is not available in the EEA, Switzerland, the United Kingdom, and some U.S. states. GIF creation itself is listed as available in the UK, so that limit applies to those extra editing and avatar tools, not to the basic loop.

## A small feature, and a familiar last mile

If the button is already in your app, this is an easy thing to try today: a three-second reaction, a looping product shot, a short explain-it clip for a message or a landing page. If it is not there yet, you are probably waiting on the rollout, or you are outside the eight countries, under 18, or on an account that does not meet Google's plan rules.

A custom GIF will not finish a site or an app for you. If you are building in Lovable, Bolt, Cursor, or v0, the last mile is still sign-in, payments, and a deploy that holds up, whether or not Gemini can already make the animation at the top of the page.
