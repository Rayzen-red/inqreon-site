On Nov. 17, Google began rolling out Tasks in Gemini free of charge in the United States, Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. The feature uses Gemini 4 Hydrogen. Google says the update will reach hundreds of millions of people. In this mode, Gemini can place calls, pay bills, and carry a task through to the end, with a set of more advanced features starting in the U.S.

To try it, you open Gemini and switch the home-screen toggle from Chat to Task. That is the whole on-ramp. The limits sit just behind it.

## What Google says it can do now

Google's post is written as a walk through the day of Josh Woodward, who leads Labs, the Gemini app, and AI Studio. In one example, he asks Gemini to call the two nearest dealerships and book a Friday morning service slot. Gemini, he writes, already knows the family's minivan and address through Personal Intelligence, finds the shops in Google Maps, calls both at once, sits through the hold music, and puts the appointment on Google Calendar. On the business's end, Gemini says right away that it is an AI. Businesses can opt out, and Gemini will not give out payment or private account information unless the user says so.

Other examples in the post include permission to use Google Password Manager so Gemini can open a utility portal and explain a $389 electric bill; shopping with a new Google Pay integration, a spending cap, and receipts in Google Wallet; and adding items to an Instacart order by text, including from a photo. Google says Gemini now uses the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2) for purchases, and that it is designed to stay within a cap you set and to ask for confirmation or pre-approval before clicking Buy. Card points and purchase protections still apply on supported cards, according to Google. It also says Gemini connects with more than 60 apps, and that you can forward emails to gemini@google.com, starting in the U.S., to put dates on a calendar.

## The limits worth reading before you hand over a bill

Calling through Gemini is not available in Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska. Texting Gemini requires a U.S. phone number with an eligible carrier. Google is frank that tasks still fail: a job may stop short or miss the mark, and on some websites Gemini gets most of the way through and then hands the screen back to you for the last click. The company says it is working with major sites so assistants and site owners can coexist.

Google also describes the security setup. Each task, it says, runs in a hardware-isolated virtual machine on Google Cloud's Agent Compute Engine, dedicated to your account, plus prompt-injection filters and Safe Browsing limits on which links Gemini can open. You can jump in with more instructions, disconnect apps, or delete chat history. For a closer look, Google points to a technical paper and to its bug bounty program.

## What to try, and what still sits with you

If you are in one of the six countries and already keep your calendar, mail, and payments in Google's products, a small, reversible task is the fair test: a call to book an appointment, a draft grocery list, or a calendar update from a forwarded email. Keep the spending cap tight, and watch for the confirmation before anything is paid.

If you run a site or an app that an assistant might need to finish a booking or a checkout on, this launch is also a reminder that the last button still has to work for a real person when the agent gets stuck.
