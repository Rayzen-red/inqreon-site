On Nov. 18, OpenAI added accounting workflows to ChatGPT. A dedicated Accounting plugin, which is free to install, is meant to help with invoice drafts, bank reconciliation, and month-end reports, using the records you already keep in tools such as QuickBooks Online and Xero. You still review the figures and approve anything before it is posted.

## What the plugin actually does

OpenAI's product page describes a familiar close cycle, not a replacement accountant. ChatGPT can prepare draft entries from invoices and receipts, suggest account coding, and flag details that look unclear. It can pull together invoices, statements, and payment records from email, files, and connected accounting apps, then compare workpapers with the supporting documents and draft review notes on possible duplicates, missing information, or unexplained differences. If your workspace has skills, you can save a template, coding rules, and review checks and reuse them the next month.

The official help article adds a narrower version of audit support: you provide the request list and the schedules or documents you are allowed to use, and ChatGPT matches each item to evidence and lists what is missing. That is a first pass at a binder, not an audit opinion.

For apps that do not have a plugin, OpenAI says computer use can still help inside the software. The company is also clear about the line you should not cross: review the supporting records, the calculations, and the suggested coding before you approve an entry or a report. A connection that can read transactions may have different permissions for writing them. If an entry's status is unclear, the help page says to check the accounting app before trying again, so you do not create a duplicate.

## How to get it, and who it is for

The Nov. 18 [release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes) are short and practical. Open the ChatGPT desktop app for macOS or Windows, set up computer use, open the Accounting plugin listing, and install it if it is available for your plan and workspace. The help center says the plugin itself is free to install, but access to the model, tools, and usage in your plan can vary.

In a ChatGPT Business workspace, members can install Accounting without prior admin approval. In an Enterprise workspace, an admin has to approve the plugin first. Workspace admins also choose which connected apps are enabled. Users connect their own accounts, and ChatGPT is supposed to respect the permissions those accounts already have. OpenAI says data from ChatGPT Business and Enterprise is not used to train its models by default.

The partner list is wider than QuickBooks and Xero. The help article names BILL, Campfire, FreshBooks, Oracle NetSuite, Oracle QuickStart, QuickBooks Online, Rillet, and Xero. Open the listing for the app you use and check which actions it actually supports.

Xero, for its part, published a follow-up on Nov. 19. President of product Diya Jolly wrote that the company is expanding its existing ChatGPT integration so accountants can start a month-end close review, a client portfolio review, or quarterly meeting prep inside ChatGPT for Accounting, then take action back in Xero, where the ledger stays the system of record. Xero says this part is currently available in the United States.

## What to try this week

If you already work in one of the supported ledgers, the useful test is a task you can check by hand: a short reconciliation, a handful of invoice drafts, or a month-end pack in your usual spreadsheet template. Ask ChatGPT to show the source row for each figure, then approve nothing until you have looked at it. The testimonials on OpenAI's page describe time saved; treat those as other people's experience, not a promise about your close.
