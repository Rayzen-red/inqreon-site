Gamma makes presentations, documents and simple websites from a prompt, an outline or a file you already have. It has quietly become one of the most visited AI tools: about 20.8 million visits in October 2026, per third-party estimates from [AlphaMoat](https://www.alphamoat.ai/en/ai/compare/gamma-app-vs-midjourney-com), and 20th place in Similarweb's [AI chatbots and tools ranking](https://www.similarweb.com/top-websites/ai-chatbots-and-tools/). For comparison, the same AlphaMoat estimate puts Midjourney at 8.6 million.

If you build pitch decks, client reports or lesson slides more than once a month, Gamma is worth a look. Here is what it does, what it costs and where you'll still want PowerPoint.

## What Gamma is

Gamma is an AI design tool for content that people read on a screen. On its [pricing page](https://gamma.app/pricing) it lists presentations, documents, websites, social posts and images. You type a topic or paste notes, and Gamma drafts a full deck with layout, text and images. Then you edit by hand or ask the built-in AI agent to change things.

Gamma says it uses models from several companies, including Anthropic's Claude, OpenAI's ChatGPT and DALL-E, Google's Gemini, and image models such as Flux, Recraft, Ideogram, Luma and Leonardo.

Useful details from the pricing page:

- **Import** from PDF and PPTX.
- **Export** to PDF, PPTX, PNG and Google Slides, even on Free.
- **Publish** as a web page, with custom domains on Pro and Ultra.
- **API access**, which Gamma says works directly or through no-code tools such as Zapier and Make.

## Where it is strong

**A good first draft, fast.** Turning a page of notes into ten clean slides used to eat an evening. Gamma gets you to a version you can react to in a few minutes, which is often the hardest part.

**Design that holds together.** Layouts, spacing and type stay consistent across slides. If design isn't your strength, the default output already looks tidy.

**Works with the tools you have.** You can import a PowerPoint or PDF, improve it in Gamma, and export it back to PPTX or Google Slides for a client who lives in Office.

**Web publishing.** A deck can become a shareable page with analytics on Pro, which helps when you want to know whether someone opened your proposal.

## Where it falls short

**Credits for the good stuff.** Gamma advertises unlimited AI creations under fair use, but the agent, advanced models and API use credits. The Help Center lists 1,500 monthly credits on Plus, 4,000 on Pro and 20,000 on Ultra. Free accounts start with 400 credits that don't refill.

**Per-seat billing on personal plans.** On Plus, Pro and Ultra, each member you add to your workspace is charged as a separate seat.

**Check your exports.** Gamma's layouts are designed for its own format. Open a PPTX export before you send it to a client: spacing or fonts may need a touch-up.

**Generic content risk.** A deck drafted from a one-line prompt reads like one. Feed it your own notes, numbers and examples, or the result will sound like everyone else's.

## What it is for

- Pitch decks and sales proposals.
- Client reports and project updates.
- Lesson slides and training materials.
- One-page websites for an event, a portfolio or a product idea.
- Turning a long PDF into a short visual summary.

## Who it suits

**Founders, consultants and salespeople** who build decks every week.

**Teachers and trainers** who need clean slides quickly.

**Marketers** who want a document, a deck and a social image from one source.

It suits less well designers who need pixel-level control, or teams locked into strict PowerPoint templates.

## Pricing and plans (as of November 2026)

The official [pricing page](https://gamma.app/pricing), checked on November 15, 2026, shows these prices for annual billing:

| Plan | Price (billed annually) | Monthly credits | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 400 at signup, no refill | Up to 10 slides per prompt, import PDF and PPTX, export to PDF, PPTX, PNG and Google Slides |
| Plus | $9/month ($108/year) | 1,500 | Up to 100 slides per prompt, remove Gamma branding, advanced image models |
| Pro | $18/month ($216/year) | 4,000 | Premium image models, custom branding, detailed analytics, up to 10 custom domains, API access |
| Ultra | $90/month ($1,080/year) | 20,000 | Most advanced text, image and video models, up to 100 custom domains, early access |

Credit numbers come from Gamma's [Help Center](https://help.gamma.app/en/articles/8077107-how-can-i-upgrade-my-gamma-subscription). Gamma says annual billing saves up to 28% compared with monthly billing, so month-to-month plans cost more; check the "Pay monthly" tab on the pricing page for those rates. The Help Center also says Ultra is offered monthly only, while the pricing page shows an annual Ultra price, so confirm the Ultra terms at checkout. Unused credits roll over up to twice your monthly amount. Business plans are priced separately. Taxes may apply depending on your location.

## Pros and cons

**Pros**

- Fast, tidy first drafts of decks and docs
- Import and export with PowerPoint, PDF and Google Slides
- Publish as a web page, with analytics on Pro
- Models from several AI companies in one tool

**Cons**

- Agent, advanced models and API run on credits
- Each added member is billed as a seat on personal plans
- PPTX exports are worth checking before you send them
- One-line prompts produce generic decks

## The short verdict

Gamma earns its keep if you make slides or documents regularly and care more about speed and a clean look than about pixel-perfect control. Start on Free with a real deck you need this week. Plus is enough for most solo users. Pro makes sense when you want custom domains, analytics or API access.
