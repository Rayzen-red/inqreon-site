Gemini is the AI assistant you may already have without signing up for anything. It sits in the Gemini app, in Gmail and Docs, in Chrome and in Google Search. In October 2026 gemini.google.com drew about 2.64 billion web visits, second only to ChatGPT, according to Similarweb data compiled by [Momentic](https://momenticmarketing.com/blog/top-ai-chatbots). Momentic also reports that the figure more than doubled from a year earlier.

If your day already runs on Google, the real question is which of the four plans is worth it. Here is how they differ and where Gemini helps or gets in the way.

## What Gemini is

Gemini is Google's family of AI models and the assistant built on them. The free app answers questions, writes, generates and edits images, runs Deep Research and offers Gemini Live for voice conversations. Paid plans, sold through Google One, add stronger models, higher limits and AI inside Google's own apps.

The model names changed a lot this year. Per the [Google AI plans page](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/), the line-up in November 2026 is:

- **Gemini 4 Hydrogen**, included from the Plus plan up.
- **Gemini 4 Argon**, which Google describes as its frontier model for complex work, from the Pro plan up.
- **Gemini 4 Carbon**, Google's strongest model, on Ultra only and in select countries.

The free app runs on a lighter Flash-Lite model.

## Where it is strong

**It lives where your files already are.** On paid plans Gemini works inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets and Vids. You can proofread an email in place, draft a doc from your notes and, in the US, ask your inbox for an overview. No copy and paste between tabs.

**Storage comes in the bundle.** Every paid plan includes Google One storage: 400 GB on Plus, 5 TB on Pro and 20 TB or more on Ultra. If you already pay Google for storage, part of the AI cost is money you were spending anyway.

**Creative tools.** Image generation with Nano Banana models, video generation through Google Flow and music with Lyria 3.5 are all part of the plans, with monthly Flow credits that grow by tier.

**Long inputs.** Pro and Ultra list a 1 million token context window. Google's subscriptions page says you can upload up to 1,500 pages of files, or a code repository of up to 30,000 lines.

## Where it falls short

**Plans change under you.** In a subscriber email published by 9to5Google, Google said that from November 13, 2026, choosing Pro models in the Gemini app and in AI Mode would only be possible on Google AI Pro and above, [Notebookcheck reports](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Google-AI-Plus-loses-Gemini-Pro-models-on-November-13.1445681.0.html). If you subscribed to Plus for Pro access, that access is gone.

**Many features are US-only.** AI Inbox, Deep Search in AI Mode, Gmail Live and Docs Live, Daily Brief and Chrome auto browse are listed for the US, or the US and India. Check the footnotes before you pay for a feature.

**Usage limits are compute-based.** Google says limits depend on the complexity of your prompt, the features you use and the length of your chat. The limit refreshes every five hours until you reach a weekly cap. You can buy AI credits to go further, but there is no simple message count.

**Work accounts can't buy these plans.** Google AI plans require a personal Google account. Companies on Google Workspace buy Gemini as a business edition instead.

## What it is for

- Writing and editing inside Gmail and Docs without leaving the page.
- Research: Deep Research browses hundreds of sources and returns a report.
- Studying: upload notes or slides and ask for a study guide or a practice test.
- Images, short videos and music for social posts or presentations.
- Coding help, including Google Antigravity, Google's agentic development platform, on Pro and Ultra.

## Who it suits

**Free** works for casual questions and occasional images.

**Google AI Plus** suits people who mainly want more storage plus Gemini 4 Hydrogen and Gemini in Gmail, and don't need the Pro model.

**Google AI Pro** is the plan for most professionals and students who live in Google apps. It is the cheapest plan with Gemini 4 Argon, the Pro model, Antigravity and Chrome auto browse.

**Google AI Ultra** is for power users, creators who generate a lot of video and anyone who wants Gemini 4 Carbon.

## Pricing and plans (as of November 2026)

US prices from the official [Google AI plans page](https://one.google.com/about/google-ai-plans/), checked on November 15, 2026, before tax.

| Plan | Price | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Flash-Lite model, image creation, Deep Research, Gemini Live, 15 GB storage |
| Google AI Plus | $4.99/month | Gemini 4 Hydrogen, 2x Free usage, video generation, 200 Flow credits, Gemini in Gmail, 400 GB |
| Google AI Pro | $19.99/month or $199.99/year | Gemini 4 Argon and the Pro model, 4x Free usage, 1,000 Flow credits, Antigravity, 1M context, YouTube Premium Lite, 5 TB |
| Google AI Ultra 5x | $99.99/month | Gemini 4 Carbon, 5x Pro usage, 10,000 Flow credits, YouTube Premium, 20 TB |
| Google AI Ultra 20x | $199.99/month | 20x Pro usage, 25,000 Flow credits, Project Genie, 30 TB |

Prices differ by country. All Google AI plans are for ages 18 and up, and Google says there are no refunds for partial billing periods unless the law requires them. For teams, Gemini Enterprise Business starts at $21 per seat per month on the [Gemini subscriptions page](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/).

## Pros and cons

**Pros**

- Built into Gmail, Docs, Chrome and Search
- Generous storage included with every paid plan
- Strong creative tools for images, video and music
- Pro plan includes a 1M token context window

**Cons**

- Plan contents shift, as Plus subscribers just found out
- Many headline features are US-only
- Limits are compute-based and hard to predict
- Personal Google accounts only

## The short verdict

If you already pay Google for storage and work in Gmail and Docs, Google AI Pro is easy to justify. Plus is now a storage plan with a capable assistant, not a cheap way into Pro models. Ultra makes sense only if you hit Pro's limits every week or need heavy video generation.
