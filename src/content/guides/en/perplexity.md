Perplexity is the AI tool built around one habit: every answer comes with its sources. In October 2026 perplexity.ai drew about 88.3 million web visits, sixth among AI chatbots, according to Similarweb data in [Momentic's roundup](https://momenticmarketing.com/blog/top-ai-chatbots). The same data shows visits down 60% from a year earlier, so it has lost ground as ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude added their own web search.

It is still the tool many researchers keep open in a tab. Here is why, what it costs and where it falls short.

## What Perplexity is

Perplexity is an answer engine. You ask a question, it searches the web, reads the pages and writes an answer with numbered citations you can click. It runs on the web, in mobile and desktop apps, and inside Comet, Perplexity's own browser.

It isn't tied to a single model. Per the [pricing page](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/pricing), Perplexity picks a model for each query from several labs, including OpenAI, Google, Anthropic and NVIDIA. The [Pro page](https://www.perplexity.ai/pro) lists models such as GPT-6.1 Sol, Gemini 4 Hydrogen, Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Grok 4.7. On paid plans you can choose your preferred model yourself.

Around search, Perplexity has added:

- **Deep Research**, for long reports built from many sources.
- **Perplexity Computer**, an agent that, in Perplexity's words, creates personalized apps, tools and documents.
- **Model Council**, which runs one question across several models.
- **Connectors** to more than 400 apps, and search across your files.

## Where it is strong

**Citations by default.** Every answer shows where each claim came from. You can click through and check, which is what you should do for anything that matters.

**Several labs in one subscription.** If you want to compare how different models answer, Pro lets you switch without paying for three or four separate plans.

**Premium data.** Perplexity says Pro and Max add premium sources for finance and market research, including PitchBook, Statista and S&P Capital IQ. Those normally sit behind their own expensive subscriptions.

**Fast for factual questions.** For "what changed," "who said" and "compare these three" questions, a search-first tool often gets you to a usable answer faster than a general chatbot.

## Where it falls short

**Sources aren't proof.** A citation shows where a claim came from, not that it's true. Perplexity can still summarize a page wrongly or lean on a weak source. Read the linked page when the stakes are high.

**Falling traffic.** The 60% year-over-year drop in web visits doesn't make the product worse, but it does show that general assistants now cover much of the same ground.

**Usage is described loosely.** The plan table on the pricing page uses words like "Limited," "10x" and "Maximum." Exact counts aren't stated on that page.

**Personal use only.** The Pro page says consumer plans are for personal use. Companies need the Enterprise plans.

## What it is for

- Quick research with links you can check.
- Market and competitor research, especially with PitchBook and Statista data on paid plans.
- Comparing answers from different AI models on the same question.
- Long reports with Deep Research.
- Building small tools and documents with Perplexity Computer.

## Who it suits

**Free** suits people who want cited answers to simple questions a few times a day.

**Pro** fits students, journalists, analysts, marketers and founders who research every day and want to choose models.

**Max** is for heavy, sustained research and agent work: it adds 10,000 monthly Computer credits, frontier models and the highest limits.

**Enterprise** is for teams that need admin controls.

## Pricing and plans (as of November 2026)

The official [pricing page](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/pricing), checked on November 15, 2026, shows prices for annual billing only:

| Plan | Price on the page | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Search with citations, basic models, limited daily use |
| Pro | $17/month, billed annually | Expanded Computer access, 4,000 bonus credits, Deep Research, top models with model selection, documents and apps |
| Max | $167/month, billed annually | Maximum Computer usage, 10,000 monthly credits, 35,000 bonus credits, frontier models, priority access to new features |

Monthly billing costs more than these annual-rate figures. The page we checked didn't print the monthly price, so look it up on the [official pricing page](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/pricing) before you subscribe. At the time of checking, Perplexity also showed a limited-time bonus of free Computer credits on both paid plans. Enterprise plans are listed on a separate tab.

## Pros and cons

**Pros**

- Citations on every answer
- Models from several labs in one plan
- Premium finance and market data on paid plans
- Deep Research and Model Council for harder questions

**Cons**

- Citations still need checking
- Web traffic down sharply year over year
- Limits described in words and multiples, not numbers
- Consumer plans are for personal use only

## The short verdict

If your work depends on checking where facts come from, Perplexity earns its place, often next to a general assistant rather than instead of one. Try Free for a week of real questions. Pay for Pro if you need model choice, premium data or Deep Research on a regular basis.
