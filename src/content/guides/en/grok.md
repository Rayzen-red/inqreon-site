Grok is the AI assistant from xAI, the company now called SpaceX Super Intelligence, or SpaceXSI. In October 2026 grok.com drew about 203.8 million web visits, fifth among AI chatbots, per Similarweb data in [Momentic's roundup](https://momenticmarketing.com/blog/top-ai-chatbots). That count covers the website only. Grok is also built into X, so the real audience is wider than the web number suggests.

The bigger change this fall is how you pay for it. Since October, Grok, the Grok Bot agent, the Cursor code editor and X Premium are sold as one plan called [The X Subscription](https://x.ai/news/x-subscription). That makes Grok an unusual buy: you may end up paying for a code editor and a social media checkmark along with your chatbot.

## What Grok is

Grok is a chat assistant with voice, image generation (Imagine), video generation and a builder mode called Grok Build. You can use it at grok.com, in the iOS and Android apps, and in the Grok tab on X. Paid plans add Grok Bot, which xAI describes as AI teammates that get work done, plus API access for developers.

On the [pricing page](https://x.ai/pricing) the latest Grok models start on Premium. The cheaper Plus plan gets Grok, but not the newest models.

## Where it is strong

**One subscription for three products.** If you already pay for X Premium or use Cursor, the bundle can save money. xAI says Premium gives you the benefits of SuperGrok, Cursor Pro and X Premium+, which it says used to cost $90 a month combined, for $30. That's xAI's comparison, not ours, but the current prices are on the page.

**Shared usage pool.** Your allowance covers Grok in the apps, the Grok tab on X and Grok models in Cursor. It refreshes weekly, so xAI says one heavy day won't wipe out your month.

**Creative tools in the same plan.** Image and video generation sit next to chat. On Super and above, xAI lists 1080p videos up to 15 seconds long.

**Live context from X.** Because Grok is built into X, it suits questions about what people are posting right now. Treat posts as opinions, not facts, and check claims elsewhere.

## Where it falls short

**You pay for things you may not use.** If you only want a chatbot, part of the price goes to X perks and Cursor. For some people that's a bonus. For others it's noise.

**Third-party models cost extra.** In Cursor, models from other companies are not part of the included usage. You buy them separately through top-ups at API list prices. Plus includes Grok models only.

**Usage is described in multiples.** Plans give "4x," "15x" or "50x" the usage of Plus, but the base amount isn't stated in plain numbers on the pricing page. You see what's left in settings.

**Tied to X.** The X features are part of the deal, and so is the connection to your X account if you want those benefits. If you don't use X, that link is irrelevant at best.

## What it is for

- Everyday chat, voice conversations and quick research.
- Following live discussions and news through X.
- Making images and short videos for posts.
- Coding in Cursor with Grok models, on the same plan.
- Handing tasks to Grok Bot on Premium and above.

## Who it suits

**Free** works for occasional questions and light image generation.

**Plus** fits X creators who want the checkmark and some AI usage. xAI itself recommends it for social creators with limited AI use.

**Premium** is the plan xAI recommends for new AI users: latest Grok models, Grok Bot, video and the Cursor features that matter for coding.

**Super and Ultra** are for daily agent users and power users, in xAI's words.

## Pricing and plans (as of November 2026)

List prices from the official [pricing page](https://x.ai/pricing) and [pricing docs](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricing), checked on November 15, 2026. Prices exclude tax.

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Usage | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | $0 | Limited | Daily Grok access, a starter allowance for Cursor agents, standard X |
| Plus | $8 | $80/year | Base | Grok Chat, Voice, Imagine and Build, Grok API access, Cursor with Grok models, X checkmark |
| Premium | $30 | $300/year | 4x Plus | Latest Grok models, Grok Bot, images and video, Cursor cloud agents and code reviews, mostly ad-free X |
| Super | $100 | $1,000/year | 15x Plus | Priority at peak times, 1080p 15-second video |
| Ultra | $200 | $2,000/year | 50x Plus | Earliest access to new products and models |

Team seats cost the same as individual tiers: $30, $100 or $200 per seat per month. Enterprise pricing is custom. API usage is billed separately at standard model rates. Existing SuperGrok, Cursor and X Premium plans continue unless you switch, and xAI says switching gives a prorated refund for the old plan.

## Pros and cons

**Pros**

- One plan covers Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor and X Premium
- Weekly usage pool shared across chat and coding
- Image and video generation included
- Built into X for live discussion

**Cons**

- You pay for X and Cursor perks even if you don't need them
- Third-party models in Cursor cost extra
- Usage tiers are multiples, not clear numbers
- Newest models start at $30 a month

## The short verdict

Grok makes the most sense if you already live on X, already code in Cursor, or both. Then Premium replaces two or three bills with one. If you only want a general assistant, compare it with ChatGPT Plus or Google AI Pro before you decide, and look at which features you'd actually open each week.
