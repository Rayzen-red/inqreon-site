Claude, made by Anthropic, had the biggest jump of any major AI assistant this year. Similarweb counted about 1.25 billion web visits to claude.ai in October 2026, up 536% from a year earlier, per [Momentic's roundup](https://momenticmarketing.com/blog/top-ai-chatbots). [Forbes Middle East](https://db.forbesmiddleeast.com/innovation/artificial-intelligence-machine-learning/claude-posts-massive-month-over-month-traffic-boost-as-anthropic-ipo-looms-1), citing Similarweb, reports a 23.8% rise in October alone. It is now the third most visited AI chatbot, behind ChatGPT and Gemini.

Claude's tools lean toward writing, code and long, careful work. Here is what Claude does well, where it can frustrate you, and how its plans compare.

## What Claude is

Claude is a chat assistant on the web, desktop and mobile. It runs on Anthropic's model families: Haiku (fast and cheap), Sonnet (the everyday workhorse), Opus (heavier reasoning) and Fable, which Anthropic calls its top model. Around the chat, Anthropic has built a set of tools listed on the [pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing):

- **Artifacts**: documents, small apps and visuals Claude builds next to the chat.
- **Claude Design, Slides and Docs**, available even on the free plan.
- **Claude Code**: a coding agent that works in your terminal and editor, on paid plans.
- **Claude in Chrome** and **Claude for Microsoft 365**, on paid plans.
- **Research**, **Projects**, memory, connectors to your apps, and the option to hand off and schedule tasks.

## Where it is strong

**Writing that sounds like a person.** Drafting and editing is where Claude feels most at home. In our use it follows tone instructions closely and handles long documents without flattening them. Test it on a text of your own before you take our word for it.

**Coding.** Claude Code is included in every paid plan and shares one usage pool with your chats. You can ask it to read a project, change several files and run tests, then review what it did.

**Long context.** The pricing page lists a context window of up to 1 million tokens, depending on the model. That is enough for a large report, a contract bundle or a mid-size codebase in one go.

**Useful free plan.** Free users get web search, file creation, code execution, memory, connectors, Artifacts and the Design, Slides and Docs tools. The free tier is meant to be used, not just tasted.

## Where it falls short

**Usage limits you feel.** Every plan has a rolling five-hour window, and paid plans add a weekly cap. Anthropic says there is no fixed message count: long chats, big files and heavier models use more. Heavy users hit the wall, and it often happens mid-task.

**No image generation in the plan list.** The pricing page covers chat, documents, code and research. If you need pictures or video, you will want a second tool.

**Top model costs extra on Pro.** Fable is available on Pro only through paid usage credits. Max and Ultra include it for up to half of your weekly limit.

**Training opt-out, not opt-in.** On Free, Pro, Max and Ultra, the plan table marks model training as "Opt-out." Team and Enterprise are "None by default." If you paste sensitive material, check your settings.

## What it is for

- Writing, editing and rewriting long texts while keeping your voice.
- Reading and comparing long documents: contracts, research papers, reports.
- Coding with Claude Code, from small fixes to multi-file changes.
- Building quick prototypes, slides and documents with Artifacts and the Claude tools.
- Research tasks with web search and the Research feature on paid plans.

## Who it suits

**Free** suits writers and students who want a careful assistant for a few sessions a day.

**Pro** is the plan for most working professionals and developers who code part-time. Claude Code, Projects, Research and Opus all start here.

**Max** fits people who use Claude all day or run Claude Code for hours. You get five times Pro's usage and Fable within your weekly limit.

**Ultra** is for the longest tasks: no five-hour limit for verified accounts and ten times Pro's weekly usage.

**Team** (2 to 150 people) and **Enterprise** are for companies.

## Pricing and plans (as of November 2026)

Figures from the [official pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing), checked on November 15, 2026. Prices exclude tax.

| Plan | Price | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Limited usage every 5 hours and every week, Sonnet and Haiku, web search, Artifacts, Design, Slides, Docs, up to 5 projects |
| Pro | $20/month, or $17/month billed annually ($200 up front) | More usage, Opus, Claude Code, Research, Projects, Claude in Chrome and Microsoft 365, Fable via usage credits |
| Max | $100/month | 5x Pro usage, Fable up to half your weekly limit, $100/month in API credits, priority at peak times |
| Ultra | $200/month | No 5-hour limit for verified accounts, 10x Pro weekly usage, $200/month in API credits |

Max and Ultra are billed monthly only. Anthropic says Claude Enterprise costs $20 per seat per month plus usage billed at API rates, on an annual contract. The Team plan has no price printed on the page we checked, so look at the [pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing) for the current per-seat cost. When you hit a limit on a paid plan, you can turn on usage credits and keep working at standard API rates.

## Pros and cons

**Pros**

- Strong at long writing, careful reading and coding
- Claude Code included in every paid plan
- Up to 1M tokens of context, depending on the model
- A free plan with real tools, not just chat

**Cons**

- Five-hour and weekly limits can stop you mid-task
- No image or video generation listed in the plans
- Fable needs extra credits on Pro
- Training is on by default for personal plans until you opt out

## The short verdict

Claude is the pick if your work is mostly words and code, and you want an assistant that reads carefully. Start on Free. If you code or write daily, Pro is the natural step. Move to Max only after you have hit Pro's weekly cap more than once.
